# Behance Case Study Migration

Use this checklist when turning a Behance project into a portfolio case-study page.

## 1. Source Inventory

- Behance URL:
- Project slug:
- Public, private, or coming soon:
- Assets to reuse:
- Assets that should not be public:
- Videos/GIFs to convert or replace:

## 2. Case Study Metadata

- Title:
- Company/client:
- Year or timeframe:
- Your role:
- Team/collaborators:
- Product area:
- Skills shown:
- Outcome or impact:

## 3. Portfolio Story Outline

Keep the story editable as HTML text instead of baking it into images.

1. Project intro
2. Context and challenge
3. Objective
4. Your role
5. Research or discovery
6. Key insights
7. Strategy and prioritization
8. Design explorations
9. Constraints and tradeoffs
10. Launch or delivery
11. Impact
12. Legacy or reflection

## 4. Content Pass

- Rewrite Behance image text into real headings, paragraphs, lists, and captions.
- Keep each section focused on one idea.
- Make the first screen answer: what this is, why it matters, and what you did.
- Replace placeholder project-card copy before publishing.
- Add useful image alt text while the asset context is fresh.
- Mark anything confidential before moving assets into `/assets`.

## 5. Review Questions

- Does this case study make sense without seeing every image?
- Is the role clear enough for a recruiter or hiring manager?
- Is the outcome specific, but still safe to share publicly?
- Are any screenshots, Slack posts, customer details, or internal metrics too sensitive?
- Can the page survive on mobile once the real content is in place?

## 6. Launch Checklist

- No placeholder titles or descriptions.
- No dead `href="#"` links.
- External links use `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Footer year is current.
- Protected work is not served by GitHub Pages as plain static files.
- Images and videos have intentional filenames and alt text.
